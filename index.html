SpongeBob SquarePants

Spongebob is a cartoon character from the show named Spongebob Squarepants. He is a yellow sponge living underwater, in his pineapple house. The show follows him and his friends on their adventures

Why I like this character

  1. Spongebob is very energetic and funny
  2. Spongebob always has different adventures in every episode
  3. Spongebob interacts with many characters with different personalities in the show
